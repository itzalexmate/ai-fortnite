# Single-Player AI-Driven Fortnite MVP

## 1. Core Concept (Value Proposition)
Deliver the **Fortnite experience**—combat, survival, and building—without relying on human players. The goal is to validate whether a player will stay engaged against AI opponents that mimic human unpredictability.

## 2. MVP Feature Set
Minimum mechanics to prove engagement value:

- Solo mode with 99 AI bots.
- One map with storm shrink and loot zones.
- Basic building: walls, ramps, and edits.
- AI with adjustable difficulty (aggressive, defensive, builder).
- Win condition: last survivor.
- Optional offline progression (XP, weapon unlocks).

## 3. AI Systems
Early MVP bots only need core combat and pathfinding:

- **Detection layer:** vision and sound triggers.
- **Decision tree:** attack, defend, build, retreat.
- **Pathfinding:** use navmesh for traversal.
- **Adaptive aim:** hit probability scales with difficulty.
- **Behavior variety:** random emotes, resource farming, or ambush logic for realism.

## 4. Validation Metrics

- **Engagement:** average match length and replay rate.
- **Satisfaction:** difficulty balance and perceived realism.
- **Performance:** how many AI units can run at stable FPS.

## 5. Expansion Path (Post-MVP)

Once validated, expand with:

- AI squads and cooperative missions.
- Story campaigns tied to existing Fortnite lore.
- AI learning modes (bots adapt to player tactics).
- Sandbox creative tools using AI companions.
- Cloud saves, daily quests, and skill tiers.

## Summary
The MVP aims to deliver a single-player survival sandbox with 99 intelligent AI bots to prove that Fortnite’s core gameplay loop remains compelling without human opponents. Positive engagement with AI-driven matches justifies scaling into story campaigns, co-op missions, or hybrid online-offline modes.
